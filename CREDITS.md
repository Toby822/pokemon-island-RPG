# Credits and data provenance

## Pokémon data and sprites

- [PokéAPI](https://pokeapi.co/docs/v2/) and its contributors.
- Species, base stats, types, evolution parents, Legendary/Mythical flags and type-effectiveness snapshot: [PokeAPI/pokeapi CSV data](https://github.com/PokeAPI/pokeapi/tree/master/data/v2/csv), retrieved 15 October 2026.
- Default species #1–#1025 are included; alternate forms are excluded.
- Pokémon sprite URLs: [PokeAPI/sprites](https://github.com/PokeAPI/sprites). Sprite images are loaded on demand and are not bundled into this repository.
- Pokémon and Pokémon character names are trademarks of Nintendo. Pokémon characters and artwork belong to their respective rights holders. No ownership of those assets is claimed.

## Networking

PeerJS 1.5.4 is vendored as assets/peerjs.min.js from its published npm distribution. [PeerJS project](https://github.com/peers/peerjs) · [Documentation](https://peerjs.com/docs/).

The upstream licence notice is retained in the distribution. Public PeerJS Cloud is used for signalling; gameplay uses WebRTC data connections. A production deployment can replace signalling/STUN configuration and provide its own TURN relay.

## Interface

- DM Sans and Manrope are requested through Google Fonts, with system-font fallbacks.
- Terrain, scenery, emblem, HUD, particles and synthesised audio are generated by this project.

## Design research

- [Hypixel SkyBlock Banking](https://wiki.hypixel.net/Banking): inspiration for carried-versus-protected currency.
- [Hypixel SkyBlock Coins](https://wiki.hypixel.net/Coins): inspiration for a repeatable combat and quest economy.
- [Pokémon Auto Chess](https://www.pokemon-auto-chess.com/): reference for a browser-based Pokémon fan-game experience.
- [PokéSurvivor overview](https://www.pokemon-auto-chess.org/en/fan-games/pokesurvivor-play): reference for the requested survivors-style combat loop.

No gameplay code or art was copied from Hypixel or Pokémon Survivors. This game uses an original implementation and custom progression; it does not claim exact mechanical parity with either.
